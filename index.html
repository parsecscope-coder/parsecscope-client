3D medical-imaging research platform

Building clearer views of medical images.

ParsecScope is building a native C++ medical-imaging engine for volumetric CT analysis, with an emphasis on transparent engineering, reproducibility and control of the core technology stack.

Research use only. ParsecScope is not clinically validated or cleared for diagnosis. Do not use this preview for patient-care decisions or upload identifiable patient data.

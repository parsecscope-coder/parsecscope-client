What to check

  1. Download an image and choose it on the client preview page. Confirm the filename, size and format recognition.
  2. In a NIfTI viewer or enabled test backend, load the matching mask as an overlay. Check alignment and label colors.
  3. Report the case ID, browser or viewer, expected result and any error message.

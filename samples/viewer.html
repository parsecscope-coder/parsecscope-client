Local NIfTI slice viewer

Select a research CT volume and, optionally, its matching reference mask. Files stay in your browser. This viewer shows stored axial slices; it does not run AI inference or apply a radiological orientation convention.

Full scans open one slice at a time. For faster browsing, extract a .nii.gz file and select the .nii file. Files remain on your device.

Choose an image to begin.

Reference labels: 1 kidney (teal), 2 tumor (orange), 3 cyst (blue). They are supplied annotations, not model predictions. Research and software testing only.
